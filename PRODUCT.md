# Product

## Name
Luna

## Purpose
A soft, supportive menstrual cycle companion that helps users log their cycle, receive gentle predictions, and chat about symptoms and feelings without clinical tone.

## Users
- Primary: people tracking their own menstrual cycles

## Core Jobs
- Log period start/end and related symptoms
- View next period and ovulation estimates
- Ask questions about cycle patterns in plain language

## Non-Goals
- Medical diagnosis or clinical advice
- Wearable integrations or partner sharing (v1)

## Voice & Tone
Warm, caring, bubbly, and supportive. Simple language, friendly reassurance, no medical claims.

## Brand Feel
Feminine-minimal, soft, airy, and calm. Spacious layouts, delicate typography, blush tones, and gentle contrast.

## Accessibility
Readable type, clear focus states, and sufficient contrast on primary text.

## Success Signals
- Users can log a cycle quickly
- Chat responses feel personal and gentle
- The UI feels calm on both desktop and mobile
